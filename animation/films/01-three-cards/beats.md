## Accuracy flags (canon check)

1. **"1 : 5.4 quintillion"** (slide 3). The founding story says this estimate "was eventually retired when we could count instead of estimate." Leave it out of the film, or replace it with the current counted figure if one exists. **[Chris]**
2. **Vertical pairs → twenty** (slide 38) comes before the forties on the slide. The story's TRUE_FIXES struck exactly that ordering. The film follows the story.
3. **The 2×2 / torus property** is credited on slide 3 to "the first day." The story credits Mr. Ehret's column swap in the first week. The film follows the story.
4. **Death/Chariot clue** (slide 48). The story says 7 and 13 "offered little I could confidently name." Drop it, or present it as weak.
5. **Slide 55, "four of everything"** (states of matter, forces, dimensions, nucleotides). This is where a scientist will push back. If we use it at all, frame it as an invitation ("fours keep showing up; check them"), not as evidence.

## Rights flag

The card scans on the slides look like a modern recolored Rider–Waite edition. Pamela Colman Smith's original
1909 art is public domain in the US; later recolorings can carry their own copyright. For a public film,
use scans of the 1909 printing, our own drawn glyphs, or the new deck.
