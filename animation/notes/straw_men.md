# Straw men we took apart (2026-10-14)

Two published Claude-animation workflows that Chris brought in as straw men, not as instructions to follow.

## A. "Paper-Cutout Storybook Collage" (Higgsfield-sponsored tutorial, dinosaur short)

**Kept:**
- A written style guide that carries the look across videos.
- A stop for review before anything costs credits.
- The sync rule: a reveal lands on its spoken word, one sentence per beat.
- Deterministic, seekable timelines.
- Loudness targets.
- The split: code draws, a generator paints.

**Dropped:**
- Comic, deflating tone and cute characters.
- Snappy pop-in motion.
- Ransom-note type.
- A storyboard made of generated images.
- Dozens of generated character assets, which are expensive and drift between shots.
- The "$10,000 production" framing.

## B. "Pip Sees the World" prompt pack (CLAUDE.md-driven tutorial)

**Kept:**
- A CLAUDE.md rulebook for the project.
- "Do not turn guesses into rules": only tested commands get written down.
- Four review stages, with the silent draft approved before sound.
- A storyboard rendered from the actual scene code.
- Drafts at 960×540 plus a contact sheet.
- Music and effects generated in code from the timing data.
- The source project packaged so the film can be rebuilt.

**Dropped:**
- The mascot as the center of the film.
- Beat-locked travel montage pacing.

## What neither had, that we need

- **Canon check:** every claim traced to a source.
- **Whole-pie read:** the script read as a scientist, a person of faith, a skeptic, a worker and a magic practitioner.
- **Privacy gate:** the founding story stays out of outside services until approved.
- **Sound derived from the structure** (pairs to 20, groups to 40).
- **A series system:** one line, three registers.
