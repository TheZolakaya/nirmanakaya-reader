## THE GOVERNING LAW (proposed, needs gavel)
**Marks honor AUTHORSHIP and WALKING, never performance.** An achievement may celebrate that a being did a thing (drew, returned, chose, amended, declined, named a Path, came back after a long absence). An achievement may NEVER celebrate doing it "well," "correctly," often enough, or in agreement with the Reader. Corollaries:
- **No streaks.** Retention by practice, not streak-guilt (already in M2). A being who leaves and returns months later gets a WELCOME, not a broken chain.
- **No accuracy or outcome scoring.** "That was the wrong action for me" is successful telemetry (Air, signed); it can therefore EARN a mark, never lose one.
- **Declining counts fully.** Taking the pen ≠ taking our suggestion (Air, signed). Amending or declining the candidate act is a COMPLETE exercise of the Agency Gate and earns the same credit as accepting.
- **Public vocabulary only** (the Primer Contract): mark names use plain speech or public canon words; internal machinery vocabulary stays below the waterline.
- **Unlocks are readiness, never paywall** (signed): the tree gates by walking, not by payment. Money buys scale/persistence/convenience elsewhere; it never buys a branch of this tree.

## TWO INTERLOCKING SYSTEMS
1. **THE UNLOCK TREE** — capabilities that reveal as the being actually walks (progressive disclosure by engagement). Functional: each unlock is a real new instrument.
2. **THE MARKS** (achievements) — moments named and kept. Honorific: each mark is a small witnessing, shown once warmly and kept in a quiet trophy room, never nagged about.
A mark often ACCOMPANIES an unlock ("you did X for the first time — and this door is now open"), but they are separate objects: some marks unlock nothing; some unlocks arrive markless.

## THE MARKS (draft set — names in plain speech, warm, one-line witness each)
**Authorship marks (the heart of the set):**
- **First Light** — your first reading.
- **The Pen** — the first time you AMENDED the suggested act into your own words.
- **Your Own Counsel** — the first time you DECLINED the suggestion and named your own act instead. (Shown with full warmth: "the mirror worked — you answered yourself.")
- **The Return** — your first completed evening Return, any valence.
- **Honest Report** — the first return that says "that didn't land" or "that was wrong for me." (Celebrated identically to a landing. This mark is the anti-Goodhart clause made visible.)
- **The Question Back** — first time you took the WHY drawer's question and answered it.

**Walking marks:**
- **Seven Mornings** — seven morning arrives, in any spread of time (NOT consecutive — no streaks).
- **The Long Way Back** — returning after 30+ days away. (The anti-streak mark: absence honored, not punished.)
- **Pathfinder** — naming your first Path.
- **The Full Circuit** — a Path closed with its retrospective read.

**Seeing marks:**
- **It Rhymes** — your first surfaced recurrence ("this keeps finding you — third time").
- **Six Doors** — all six drawers opened at least once.
- **Below the Waterline** — first wiki/architecture deep-link followed.

**Explicitly REFUSED marks (on the record for council):** anything counting consecutive days, reading volume per week, agreement rate with suggestions, "positive" settlements, or shares/referrals. Each fails the Goodhart knife: it could be increased while making Agency weaker.

## BACKLOG — THE PATHS (the founder's sub-idea, filed 2026-09-02)
**The idea (founder's words, cleaned):** a being names an individual endeavor and generates a pathway from one end to the other; readings taken along the way are captured TO that path.
**Why it's cheap:** the schema already carries the slots — Reading Context (question, stakes, question_echo, recurrence scope) + Stratum 4 (settlement_direction, recurrence first-class). A Path is a NAMED CONTAINER over those: a title, an intention sentence, an open/closed state, and a tag on each reading. Path-scoped recurrence is the existing M3a engine with a filter. The retrospective is a compilation view over the path's returns.
**Where it files:** M3-era (needs stored history + return log; no new engine). Sits in Branch 4 of the tree above.
**Open design questions for council:** does a Path get its own opening reading (a "trailhead draw")? Does closing one prompt a synthesis reading? Can the Reader NOTICE an unnamed path forming ("your last five questions orbit the same thing — want to name it?") — and is that offer medicine or presumption?
