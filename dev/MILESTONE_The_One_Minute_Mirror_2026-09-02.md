## THE MVP IN ONE SENTENCE
**A mirror that hands you one thing:** one accurate sentence about you, one reason it fits, one thing to do about it — in under a minute, pretty enough to feel like a gift. Everything else in Nirmanakaya is drill-down from that moment, never prerequisite to it.

## THE PRODUCT THESIS (three laws, all gaveled or signed)
1. **The iceberg law:** the ten-million-factor engine buys the one sentence; four-dimensional machinery works backend and never speaks its own vocabulary at the surface. Compression is the evidence of understanding.
2. **The utilization-unlocks law (founder, 2026-09-02):** capabilities unlock through use. Start with one card. Depth, spreads, layers, and instruments reveal as the person actually walks — progressive disclosure by engagement, not by settings pages.
3. **The two-WHYs law:** the product answers "why did you say this" fully (evidence drawer) and never answers "why does this matter to my life" — that drawer asks back. The Agency Gate is a UI node, not a disclaimer.

## THE ENGAGEMENT LADDER (the through-line of value at every rung: you, seen truly, handed the pen)
| Rung | Time | Experience | The wow | Unlocks |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. The Mirror | 60 sec | One card → WHAT / WHY-IT-FITS / WHAT-NOW on one screen | "That's accurate. And I know what to do." | (entry — no account needed for first taste) |
| 2. The Walk | daily | The practice loop: act + Choose + evening Return, logged | "This thing walks WITH me." | return log; more spreads |
| 3. The Pattern | weeks | Recurrence engine: "this signature keeps finding you — third time" | "I NEED to know that about me." | history views; pattern synthesis |

Every rung sells the next; none requires it. The mother lives on 1–3 fully served; the edge-dweller reaches 5 because 1 was accurate enough to trust.

## SURFACE CONTRACTS (from the schema, verbatim law)

- **MINIMUM_PRACTICE_SURFACE:** the mirror five + the Choose hand-back + the Return prompt (+ Way line, + Plain Answer).
- Everything else: drawer (the six questions) or backend.

### M1 — THE ONE-MINUTE MIRROR (the flagship build)
**The screen:** question in (or a "what's alive?" tap) → THE FLIP (the one gorgeous moment — card animation, the seal's geometry as visual language, sound optional; this is where pretty/sparkle lives, nowhere else) → one clean screen:
- **WHAT:** the signature sentence + verdict headline (plain answer register; question_echo woven in — the being quoted back).
- **WHY IT FITS:** one paragraph (evidential why — the fit, in their coin).
- **WHAT NOW:** the PRACTICE block per the gaveled five: candidate act (act-class grammar, today-sized) + Way line ("this act walks Free Will — feel now") + the **Choose** line ("what response here is actually yours?") + the Return prompt ("tonight, notice what came back").
- Six quiet drawer handles beneath: WHERE · HOW · WHAT · WHO · WHEN · WHY (the why-drawer asks back).
**Defaults:** one card (gaveled); stakes-aware voice rails; no jargon above the waterline. **A/B at beta (Air):** WHAT→WHY-FITS→WHAT-NOW vs WHAT→WHAT-NOW→WHY-on-tap — the ladder is a default progression HYPOTHESIS; instrument what users actually reach for next. **Scope discipline:** M1 is mostly SUBTRACTION AND ARRANGEMENT — the verdict engine, plain answer, corrections, and practice spec all exist; the build is the new surface + the practice compilation + drawers re-hung.
